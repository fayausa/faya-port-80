# Brainstorming

## Central idea
From bridging gaps to removing them: connect existing capabilities rather than create another silo.

## Questions
- What can we actually build together?
- What can each stakeholder contribute immediately?
- What pilots can be launched with existing resources?
- What can government enable without controlling?
- How do we keep students at the centre?

## Themes
1. AI Evolve: how AI reshapes the future of work, learning and governance.
2. Decentralisation and collaboration: make expertise, mentors, projects and infrastructure available across boundaries.
3. Action: the Rise Together Compact, pilots and named owners.

## Candidate interventions (proposals)
- Industry and faculty immersion
- Cross-campus, industry-defined problems and internships
- Proof-of-work learning recognition
- AI research and innovation opportunities
- Community-supported learning
- 30/90/180-day follow-ups

## Working principles
The Purple Movement convenes. Government facilitates. Industry contributes opportunities. Academia opens learning and research. NGOs and communities extend reach. Students co-create. μLearn helps coordinate opportunity and proof of work.

## Unresolved
Which partners will own which pilots? How will commitment tracking work? Which policy interventions are realistic?
